How To Play

Goal

Knock the puck into your opponent's goal. First to 7 points wins (casual length is adjustable to 3/5/7 in Settings; ranked is always 7).

Controls

Desktop — Player 1: move the mouse over the table, or use ↑↓←→ arrow keys.
Multiplayer — Player 2: WASD keys.
Mobile: drag your finger on your half of the table. In multiplayer, each player drags their own half at the same time (two-finger play).
Shortcuts: Esc/P pause · R restart · M mute.
The game auto-pauses if you switch tabs, and a stalled puck is automatically nudged back into play.

Rules

Paddles cannot cross the center line and cannot leave the table. After every goal a short countdown resets the puck to center.

Game Modes

Single Player — compete against an adaptive AI.
Local Multiplayer — two players share one device.
Online With a Friend — play across devices in a private room. One player creates a room and shares the 4‑letter code; the other joins it. Requires a signaling server URL (see the project's server setup). Skills are disabled online for fairness.
Practice — free play vs the AI with no scoring; great for testing a loadout.

Difficulty

Easy — slow paddle, delayed reactions, loose aim.
Medium — balanced speed with moderate puck prediction.
Hard — fast reactions and advanced trajectory prediction.

Ranked

Climb an MMR ladder from Bronze → Master. Win to gain rating, lose to drop; the swing scales with the score margin and the AI gets tougher as you rise.

Skills & Challenges

Unlock abilities and cosmetics by completing in-match challenges (shown in the Skills locker). Equip 1 active ability, up to 2 passive boosts, plus paddle, puck, trail and arena skins. Trigger your active ability with Space / E or the on-screen button. Skills are active in Single Player and Ranked.

Cosmetics & Profile

Arena skins follow your rank automatically (or pin one in the locker). Track career stats and achievement progress on the Profile screen.
